AIAA Professor Kirti “Karman” Ghia Memorial Award

Inaugural recipient, awarded by the AIAA Fluid Dynamics Technical Committee at SciTech 2025 for the best student paper, “Comparison of several neural network-enhanced sub-grid scale stress models for mesoscale hurricane boundary layer flow simulation” (AIAA 2025-2212). Listed among AIAA's 2025 best professional and student papers in Aerospace America and marked as a Best Paper on AIAA Aerospace Research Central.
